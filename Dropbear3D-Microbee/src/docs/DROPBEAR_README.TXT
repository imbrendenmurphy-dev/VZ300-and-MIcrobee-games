DROPBEAR 3D for the Microbee Premium Plus (1MB)
VIBED BY BPM 2026

Type DROPBEAR at the A> prompt.

Drop bears have overrun the outback. Fight through nine
connected areas, beat Big Fella, the Bunyip and the Yowie,
and get back to your ute. Twenty opals, if you're the type.

ARROWS / W A S D  walk and turn     Z X  sidestep
SPACE             blast             E    open, pull, prod
M map   P pause   R fast/sharp walls   C colour/mono

Walk into the pink arrows to move between areas; each area
remembers what you did. Coloured doors want their key,
levers swing the gates, and beating a big one opens its gate.
Knocked down? You get up at the door you came in by.
