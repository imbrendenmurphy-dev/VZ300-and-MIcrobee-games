KABOOMERANG  (engine build 0.1)
Microbee Premium Plus, 1MB RAM, 32K PCG. VIBED BY BPM 2026

At the A> prompt type:  KABOOM
Then press C for a colour monitor or M for monochrome.

Keys: arrows or Q A O P move, SPACE drops a gumnut bomb,
X (or Z or B) throws the Kaboomerang, H pauses, S sound on/off,
ESC back to the title. A joystick works too: fire drops a bomb,
or throws the boomerang when no bomb can go down.

Beat all the wildlife, then walk onto the hollow log.
