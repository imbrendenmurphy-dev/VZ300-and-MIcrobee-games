GRAVES 'N' GARGOYLES - Sir Tinplate's Long Night
A gothic arcade platformer for the Dick Smith VZ300
VIBED BY BPM 2026

In the kingdom of Dreadmoor, the sorcerer Morgrave has stolen the seven
Sunstones. The sun will not rise and the dead walk the land. Only one knight
is still awake: Sir Tinplate, the least impressive knight in the realm, in
borrowed tin armour. What could go wrong?

Fight through seven haunted stages, each with its own creatures, music and
boss: Gallows Hill, the Witchwood, the Drowned Village, the Swamp of the
Damned, the Crystal Deeps, the Demon Castle and, at last, Morgrave's Citadel.
Win better armour - from UNDIES and TIN up through STEEL, ENCHANTED and
ROYAL to LEGENDARY - and watch it fly off piece by piece when you're hit.
Find mightier weapons, throw lances, daggers, axes and torches, power them
up, and charge up magic. Bring back the dawn.

FEATURES
- 7 scrolling stages, 7 bosses, 49 creatures, each with its own behaviour:
  zombies that rise when you step near, skeletons that fight on without
  their heads, phantom brides that fade out and reappear behind you, magic
  mirrors that fire back every time you throw, and more
- 6 armour levels, drawn layer by layer on the knight; each suit takes five
  knocks (watch the red health bar) before it breaks
- 4 weapons with power-ups, plus magic
- Mini-bosses, treasure chests, checkpoints, secrets off the beaten path
- Two-channel beeper music: a gothic organ title theme and a slow,
  suspenseful tune for every stage and boss
- Passwords for every stage, 3 difficulty levels (Casual, Normal, Expert),
  continues, a high-score table and an attract mode with a playing demo
- Keyboard or joystick

TECH SPECS
Machine        Dick Smith VZ300 (Z80A, 3.55 MHz PAL)
Requires       BennVenn SD loader + 128K SRAM expansion
Display        Mode 1 (128 x 64, 4 colours); stages 5 and 7 use the second
               colour set (buff, cyan, magenta, orange)
Frame rate     about 25 fps during play
Scrolling      smooth, 2 pixels at a time, through levels about 11 screens long
Video          flicker-free and snow-free: each frame is built off-screen and
               copied to video RAM by the frame interrupt (IM2) during the
               vertical blank, with an unrolled stack copy
Sound          two pulse-wave channels mixed on the 1-bit speaker at about
               27.5 kHz, with pulse-width volume envelopes, plus sound effects
Loading        GRAVGARG.VZ loads the engine; the title pack and each stage
               pack are read from the SD card as you go (under a second each)
Code           about 9,800 lines of hand-written Z80 assembly, plus about
               8,000 lines of graphics, map and music data generated by
               Python tools

MEMORY USE
Main RAM ($7B00-$F9FF, 31.75K usable - the BennVenn DOS owns $FA00 up):
  $7B00-$7FFF   1.25K   HUD buffer, lookup tables, stack, interrupt table
  $8000-$CAF1  18.7K    resident engine, shared graphics, sound and text
  $CB00-$E2E0   6.0K    pack area: title pack or one of 7 stage packs
                        (5.5-6.0K each: tiles, map, creatures, boss,
                        music, behaviours)
  $E2E1-$ED8A   2.7K    variables and the knight's armour sprites, built
                        at run time
  $EE00-$F7FF   2.5K    off-screen frame buffer
  $F800-$F9FF   0.5K    SD sector buffer
  Free: 131 bytes

128K SRAM, bank B ($4000-$67FF, 10K):
  5.25K  two pre-rendered background ring buffers (one per scroll phase)
  3.0K   frame-copy routine, high-score code, hit counting
  1.7K   knight's layered armour data, attract mode and bestiary code,
         health bar
  Free: 34 bytes

On the SD card: 9 files, 69K in total
  GRAVGARG.VZ (23.5K) + GNGFRONT.DAT + GNGSTAG1-7.DAT

Screens are shown as the VZ displays them, at 4:3.
