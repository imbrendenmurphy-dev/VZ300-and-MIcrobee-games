GRAVES 'N' GARGOYLES - Sir Tinplate's Long Night      v0.3.13
For the Dick Smith VZ300 with the BennVenn SD loader + 128K SRAM
VIBED BY BPM 2026

INSTALLING
Copy all nine files from the SD folder into the same folder on your SD card
(the root is simplest), then run GRAVGARG.VZ from the BennVenn menu:
  GRAVGARG.VZ    the game engine
  GNGFRONT.DAT   title, menus, story and bestiary
  GNGSTAG1.DAT - GNGSTAG7.DAT   the seven stages
The game loads the .DAT files itself as you play, so keep them together.
Without the 128K SRAM it shows "NEEDS 128K SRAM".

CONTROLS
  Keyboard        Joystick            Action
  O / P           left / right        walk
  Q               up or button 2      jump (hold for higher)
  A               down                crouch
  SPACE           fire                throw; hold to charge magic
  W or RETURN                         change weapon
  M                                   cast magic
  H                                   pause
  S                                   music on/off

PLAYING
- Each suit of armour takes three knocks (the red bar, top right). Then a
  piece flies off and the bar refills. Lose the last of it and you're in
  your undies; three more knocks and you lose a life.
- Armour pickups repair your armour, or upgrade it if it isn't damaged.
- Walk into a treasure chest to open it, then catch what pops out.
- The lance never runs out. Daggers (60), axes (30) and torches (25) do:
  the number by the weapon icon is how many throws are left. When one runs
  out you go back to the weapon you had before. Find the same weapon again
  to enchant it and top it up.
- Checkpoints save your place in a stage. After each boss you get a
  password for the next stage; enter it from PASSWORD on the menu.
- Stages 2 to 7 each hide a secret: climb the stone wall before a row of
  spikes and leap for the high ledge beyond it.
