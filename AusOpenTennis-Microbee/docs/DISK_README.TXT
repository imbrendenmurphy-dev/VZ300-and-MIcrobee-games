MICROBEE AUSTRALIAN OPEN TENNIS
for the Microbee Premium Plus (1MB RAM, 32K PCG RAM)
VIBED BY BPM 2026

The Microbee edition of TENNIS II - GRAND SLAM (VZ300).

TO PLAY:  boot this disk, then type  AOT  at the A> prompt.
          Press C for a colour monitor or M for monochrome.

KEYS:     Q A M ,  or the arrow keys or a joystick: move
          SPACE, SHIFT or fire: hit harder (a smash if the ball is high)
          pull back as you hit: lob    push up: drop shot
          hold left/right as you hit: steer the shot
          SERVE: fire to toss, fire again to hit
          P pause (X quits while paused)   S sound
          Two players: player 2 uses I K J L + H, or the joystick

The Summer Tour (Kooyong, Perth, Sydney, then the Australian Open at
Melbourne Park) saves itself to SAVE.DAT on this disk after every
match. Choose CONTINUE TOUR to carry on. Settings and the Hall of Fame
are saved there too. Press C on the main menu to unlock the CLASSIC
1984 court.
