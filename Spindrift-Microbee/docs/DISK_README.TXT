SPINDRIFT - RIDE THE SPIRAL HOME
An arcade shooter for the Microbee Premium Plus.  VIBED BY BPM 2026

TO PLAY
  At the A> prompt type:   SPIN
  Press C for a colour monitor or M for a monochrome one.
  Needs a Premium Plus with 1MB RAM and 32K PCG RAM.

THE IDEA
  Your ship rides the rim of a vortex. The swarm spirals out of deep
  space at its centre: far away they are specks, close up they are big.
  Sweep round the rim and shoot into the middle.

KEYS                              JOYSTICK
  Z            sweep clockwise      stick: fly round to that side
  X            sweep anticlockwise  fire:  shoot
  arrows/QAOP  fly round to that side of the vortex
  SPACE        fire
  H  pause   S  sound (music+fx / fx / off)   R  restart   ESC  quit

PLAYING
  An enemy that flashes is about to break away and attack you.
  You have three ships, each with a three-hit shield.
  Shoot a whole group of eight for a formation bonus (it grows).
  Hits on attackers score double; point blank at the rim, double again.
  Get through a wave untouched for a no-damage bonus.
  Every second sector ends with a Guardian: break its pod ring, then
  shoot the eye while it is open. Beat it for the twin cannon and the
  Chance Stage: 40 jewels, 100 each, 10000 for all of them.
  Extra ships at 20000, 60000, then every 60000.

EIGHT SECTORS
  Oort Drift, Comet Lanes, Ring Works, Cinder Gate,
  Gas Giant, Iron Belt, Corona, The Eye. Then round again, harder.
