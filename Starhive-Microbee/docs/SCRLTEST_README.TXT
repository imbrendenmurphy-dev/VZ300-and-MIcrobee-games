STARHIVE - SCROLL TEST            VIBED BY BPM 2026
Microbee Premium Plus (1MB), 40 x 24 mode, 8 x 11 cells

Type SCRLTEST at the A> prompt. Loading takes a few seconds.

What to look for:
 - The starfield and the space station scroll down SMOOTHLY,
   one or two lines at a time, with no row-sized jumps.
 - The score line at the top stays perfectly still.
 - The top and bottom edges of the playfield stay still
   (scenery is cut cleanly; nothing bobs up and down).
 - Far blue stars drift at half speed (parallax).
 - The picture is steady on the monitor (no rolling).

Keys:  Q A O P, cursor keys or joystick = fly the Hornet
       SPACE = fire
       1 = slow scroll, 2 or 3 = normal, 4 = fast, 0 = stop
       W = the drone formation on/off
HUD:   score (a frame counter), map row, and IDLE = spare time
       in the last frame (bigger is better).

Reset to leave.
