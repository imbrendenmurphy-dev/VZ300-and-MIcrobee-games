THE LANTERN DEEP  -  the whole Deep: five regions, twenty rooms
An original dungeon adventure for the Microbee Premium Plus
VIBED BY BPM - 2026

REQUIREMENTS
  Microbee Premium Plus with 1MB RAM, booting the Microbee CP/M on this
  disk (or your own Premium CP/M disk with this one in a drive).

STARTING
  Boot this disk: it goes straight to the plain CP/M A> prompt (there
  is no Microbee shell menu on it). Then type:   LANTERN
  The loader fills the expanded RAM banks (dots show progress) and the
  game takes over the machine. Reset to return to CP/M.

  First it asks which monitor you have: press C for colour (or RETURN),
  M for a monochrome monitor (green, amber or white). Monochrome mode
  shows the lantern's light by the character patterns alone, so it
  fades properly on a mono screen. Creatures and treasure in good
  light are drawn as solid blocks so they stand out; at the dim edge
  of the light they are only outlines in the gloom.

KEYS
  Arrow keys, or Q A O P (W S also up/down) ... walk. Your lantern
                                                 lights the way you face.
  SPACE or Z ... strike with your sword; open, read, take and touch
  F or X     ... hurl a fire bolt (uses one magic orb)
  RETURN     ... choose on the title menu
  M          ... your journal: a map of the region, what you carry;
                 press S there to save your journey to disk
  ESC        ... back to the title (CONTINUE takes you back in)
  A Microbee joystick works too (fire = strike).

THE STORY
  Beneath the old town lie the halls of the Vaunar, who kept a great
  Lantern burning at the Heart of the Deep to hold back the Unlight.
  The Lantern has gone out. You carry the last flame down to rekindle it.

THE WAY DOWN
  1 THE DROWNED TEMPLE      find the Iron Key; it opens the way down
  2 THE OSSUARY             the Bone Colossus guards the Glimmer Lantern,
                            whose light reaches further and finds what
                            is hidden
  3 THE CRYSTAL CAVERNS     the Moonsilver Blade cuts the singing stone
  4 THE EMBER HALLS         the Ember Ring lets you cross burning rock
  5 THE HEART OF THE DEEP   face the Hollow King, rekindle the Heart

  Read the tablets. Touch the great crystals to restore your magic.
  Chests hold flasks of light and heart crystals. Rooms remember what
  you have explored, opened and defeated.

LIVES
  You have three lives (the figure and number at the bottom right). Fall,
  and you wake where the current region began, keeping everything you
  carry, with every gate, chest and boss as you left them. Lose the last
  one and the title returns: CONTINUE starts you at that region's
  beginning with three lives again.

SAVING
  Press M for your journal, then S to save your journey to disk. The
  game disk must be in drive A or B and not write-protected. The save
  goes into SAVE.DAT on this disk: the game checks that file's signature
  before writing, so nothing else on the disk is ever touched. You
  resume at the doorway where you last entered a room, with everything
  you carried, every gate, chest and boss as you left them, and the
  explored halls still remembered.

ATTRACT TOUR
  Leave the title screen alone for about 30 seconds and a demo tours the
  Deep: the Lanternless Way and the Drowned Hall, the Bone Colossus, the
  Prism Grotto, the Magma Bridge, the Forge and the Hollow King's hall.
  Any key returns to the title. (It only plays when no journey is in
  memory, so it can never disturb one.)

CONTINUE
  CONTINUE on the title menu returns you to your journey: the one in
  memory if the machine has stayed on, otherwise the one saved on disk.

FILES

  GFX.BNK      sprites and font    WORLD.BNK / WORLD2.BNK  lantern ray-trees
  REGION0-4.BNK  each region's tiles, light ramps and animations
  ROOMS0-4.BNK   each region's rooms
  TITLE.BNK    title illustration  AUDIO.BNK  sampled speech

  CCP.SYS      CP/M command processor (from the system on this disk)

STATUS
  Tested in a software model of the Premium Plus (Z80 core with the
  1MB banking, video/PCG/colour RAM, 6545, keyboard, speaker and disk
  controller), including booting this disk with the Microbee boot ROM
  into CP/M, typing LANTERN, and a scripted play-through from the first
  room to the ending. It has NOT been run on real hardware yet. The 1MB
  bank decoding follows the ubee512 Premium Plus model; LANTERN.COM
  checks RAM blocks 0-25 before loading and says so if they are not as
  expected.
