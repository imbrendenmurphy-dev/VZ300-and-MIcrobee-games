BIX THE BILBY - THE STAR DIGGER
Microbee Premium Plus: five worlds, fifteen stages
(1MB RAM, 32K PCG)            VIBED BY BPM 2026

At the A> prompt type:   BIX

M = monochrome monitor, C = colour, then SPACE.

O P or LEFT RIGHT   run
SPACE or M          jump (hold for higher)
A or DOWN           dig in (standing on earth)
O P Q A / arrows    tunnel underground
hold SPACE          underground: burst out
S                   music on / off
ESC                 give up
A joystick works too (fire = jump, down = dig).

Stomp the ants, toads and grubs, or burst up
through them. Thorns and magpies hurt. Water puts
you back on the bank for a heart.

Five worlds, three stages each: the level, the
way back (right to left), then the boss.
1 Gumleaf Gully    King Cane: stomp him
2 Crystal Caverns  Geode Golem: burst up under him
3 Clockwork Works  Rust-o-Tron: stomp it when it lands
4 Skyward Isles    Thunderhawk: stomp it when it lands
