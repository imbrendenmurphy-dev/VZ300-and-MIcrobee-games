DRACULA'S CASTLE II - THE CASTLE AWAKENS
Microbee Premium Plus (1MB RAM, 32K PCG). Vibed by BPM 2026.

Boot DRACULA.DS8, then type DRACULA.
C = colour, M = one-colour monitor (also on the title and while paused).

W/I/up forward. A/J/left, D/L/right turn. S/K/down turn around.
SPACE/RETURN act. M map. E inventory. P pause. Q quit (paused).
N music on/off. - and + music volume.
Joystick on PIO port A. Saves are RAM checkpoints, lost at power-off.

Emulator tested only - not yet run on a real Microbee.
