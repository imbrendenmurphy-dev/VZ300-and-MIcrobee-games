ROGUE MUSTER
Microbee Premium Plus, 1MB RAM, 32K PCG. VIBED BY BPM 2026

At the A> prompt type:  MUSTER

Koolabah Station, 2186. The mustering machines have gone rogue.
Jo Brumby has a welder and the station hands need bringing home.

Move:  W A S D  (or a joystick)
Fire:  I J K L  or the arrow keys - hold to keep firing
       SPACE or joystick fire shoots the way Jo was facing,
       and keeps that aim while held, so you can back away.
P pause, M sound (all / effects / off), ESC back to the title.

Walk into a station hand to rescue them: 1000, 2000 ... 5000.
Each life has 3 charge cells; a hit costs one.
Extra life every 25000.
