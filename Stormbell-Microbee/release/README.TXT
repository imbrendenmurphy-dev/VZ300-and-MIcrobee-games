STORMBELL 1.3 - VIBED BY BPM 2026
An original martial arts duelling game for the Microbee Premium Plus.

To play: at the A> prompt type  STORM  and press RETURN.
It loads in about 20 seconds.

Needs a Premium Plus (or a Premium with 1MB RAM and 32K PCG).
The picture is 40 x 25 cells of 8 x 10 (320 x 250), a full PAL screen.

COLOUR OR MONOCHROME MONITOR
  Press M on the title screen to switch. The monochrome scenery
  is drawn for amber, green and white monitors.

CONTROLS
  D / RIGHT  advance        A / LEFT  retreat
  S / DOWN   guard (hold)   K         punch
  L / UP     kick           P         pause
  ESC        quit to title  SPACE     start / try again
  A joystick works too: left/right move, down guards,
  fire punches, up kicks.
  Between duels hold D to run along the road.

Five duels on the mountain road to the silenced bell.
Watch each opponent's rhythm. Spacing and timing win;
mashing the buttons gets you punished.
