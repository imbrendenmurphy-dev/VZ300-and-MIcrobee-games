RUNE AMOK - The Vault of the Hollow King
A first-person dungeon crawl for the Microbee Premium Plus.
Run amok in the Runedeep: four depths, three sigils, one oath.
VIBED BY BPM 2026

Needs a Premium Plus with 1MB RAM. At the A> prompt type:

    RUNEAMOK

Loading takes about 16 seconds. In play, ESC (then Y) returns to the title; ESC on the title returns to CP/M.

For a thousand years the Wardens kept the Hollow King sealed
in the Runedeep. The order is dust; you are its last, half-
trained apprentice. Find the three sigils that seal his vault,
break the seal on the fourth depth and end him.

Keys
  Arrows or W A S D   walk forward/back, turn left/right
  Q E (or Z X)        step aside
  SPACE or RETURN     fight, open doors, pull levers, read,
                      drink from fountains, trade
  1                   FIREBOLT (3 magic) - once learnt
  2                   MEND (4 magic) - once learnt
  3 or P              drink a healing potion
  H                   help
  T                   music+effects / effects / silence
  ESC                 leave to the title (asks first); on the title, CP/M
  A joystick on the parallel port works too.

Turning is free; every other action takes a turn, and then the
creatures that are awake move or strike.

Hints: read the carved tablets. Some walls are only shadow.
Runes must be touched in the right order. The trader takes gold.
