# HAMBURGER SAM II — THE BURGER RUSH
### for the Dick Smith VZ200 / VZ300 — ViBED by BPM 2026

**Order up!** Sam's back behind the grill, and this time the whole town wants a burger.

Run the girders and climb the ladders. Stomp across every bun, patty, cheese slice and lettuce
leaf until it sags and drops, and cascade them floor by floor onto the plates below. Build every
burger to clear the kitchen.

Mr Hot Dog, Mr Egg and Mr Pickle are still out to stop you, and now **Mr Chilli** joins them:
fast, red and too hot to handle. Shake pepper in their faces to stun them. Drop a patty on their
heads for a satisfying **SPLAT**. Or wait until they're standing on an ingredient and give them
a ride all the way down for big bonus points. Grab the coffee for more pepper, snack on fries and
ice cream, and find the **golden spatula** that stuns the whole gang.

Cook your way through **eight kitchens**: Sam's Diner, the Cheese Factory, the Pickle Pantry, the
late-night Night Grill, the Salad Bar, the Fry-Up Factory, the Midnight Feast and the Burger Palace.
Between kitchens, catch falling layers in burger order in the **ORDER UP!** bonus round. Serve all
eight and the chasers come back faster for Lap 2.

And if you remember the 1984 original, press **C**. The classic kitchen is waiting.

- 8 kitchens + the Classic 1984 kitchen
- 4 chasers, 4 goodies, rider and squash bonuses
- ORDER UP! bonus rounds
- Top Ten cooks with 5-letter names, saved in SRAM
- Two-voice beeper music and effects
- Keyboard (the original's keys) or joystick

Requires: VZ300 or VZ200 with the BennVenn SD loader + 128K.
