EMPIRES : THE LIVING WORLD
An original civilisation and city-building game
for the Microbee Premium Plus.   VIBED BY BPM 2026

STARTING
  Needs a Microbee Premium Plus with 1MB RAM.
  Boot this disk. It goes straight to the A> prompt. Type:  EMPIRES
  The loader fills the expanded RAM (dots show progress).
  Press C for a colour monitor or M for a monochrome monitor.
  On the title, 1 starts a new game: choose one of seven tales.
  FREE PLAY uses the world type (2) and size (6, 128 x 128 or
  256 x 256) set on the title; 7 loads a saved world.  ESC in the game returns to the title; ESC on the title
  returns to A>.

KEYS
  Mouse (Microsoft serial mouse on the serial port), arrow keys,
  W A S D, I J K L or a joystick ... move the cursor
  Left button builds, right button picks the next tool.
                                (hold SHIFT to move faster)
  SPACE or RETURN ... build the selected tool
  1 Road  2 Hut  3 Farm  4 Woodcutter  5 Quarry  6 Well
  7 Market  8 Windmill  9 Watchtower  0 Clear land   TAB cycles
  M world map   C colour/monochrome   H help
  B more tools (mine, school, harbour, hospital, temple,
    university, Grand Forum - learn them first)
  R research: choose what your scholars study
  E envoys: gifts, trade and alliances with other peoples
  V goals: your tale's goal and the five paths to victory
  F save or load your world (3 slots, SAVE1-3.EMP on this disk)
  P pause   , slower   . faster

TIPS
  Farms plough the grass around them into fields. Fields yield most
  in autumn and nothing in winter, so store food for the snow.
  Huts with a road beside them, a well nearby and happy people grow
  into houses; houses near a market grow into manors.
  You can only build near your own buildings: watchtowers see far
  and widen your lands. Explore to find ancient ruins.

TALES (SCENARIOS)
  FREE PLAY               any of the five paths; play on for ever
  THE FIRST SETTLEMENT    gentle start, no rivals: grow to 50 people
  THE GREAT ISLAND        islands: 60 people and a trade agreement
  THE DIVIDED CONTINENT   three rivals: settle a third of the world
  THE LOST VALLEY         harsh land: grow to 80 people
  THE AGE OF DISCOVERY    a vast world: meet every people, explore
  THE GREAT EMPIRE        a rich realm: build culture

THE FIVE PATHS TO VICTORY (see them any time with V)
  PROSPERITY  150 people, 1000 gold and 8 arts learned
  SCIENCE     all 13 arts learned
  CULTURE     the Grand Forum, 100 people and 75% happiness
  DIPLOMACY   an alliance with every people in the world
  DOMINION    settle 30% of the world (15% of a large one) and
              raise 100 buildings
  Conquest is never needed. If your town empties, the tale ends.

OTHER PEOPLES
  Rival towns (magenta, cyan or red roofs) grow by themselves. You
  meet them when your land reaches them. Gifts make them friendlier;
  with a market and a friendly people you can agree to trade (gold
  every month); a loyal trading partner may become an ally (learning
  every month). Warlike peoples may declare war and raid your stores:
  two watchtowers turn raiders back.

MOUSE.COM
  Type MOUSE to test the serial mouse.  It shows the CPU clock, then
  twice a second how often each input bit of the two PIO ports changed:
  moving the mouse should make B4 count (B7 always counts - it is the
  screen frame signal).  Each mouse packet it decodes is printed with
  its movement and buttons.  Keys 2 and 5 switch PIO B outputs 2 and 5
  on and off, in case your mouse takes its power from those lines.
  Any other key returns to A>.
  In EMPIRES a small arrow appears at the top right of the screen once
  the mouse has been heard.
