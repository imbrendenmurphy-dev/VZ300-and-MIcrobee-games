THE FORGOTTEN DEPTHS                                         version 1.3
An atmospheric dungeon adventure for the Dick Smith VZ300
with BennVenn SD loader + 128K RAM               VIBED BY BPM - 2026
=========================================================================

FILE - copy this ONE file to the SD card (DEPTHS.DAT is no longer used)
  DEPTHS.VZ    the whole game, 20.5 KB, loads at $8000-$D1FF, autostarts
  LOAD"DEPTHS"

  Tested in my headless VZ300 emulator (an automatic play-through of
  the whole game, start to finish). NOT yet run on real hardware.

THE GAME
  Beneath the village of Ashford lie halls older than memory. The Ember
  their builders guarded is dying. Take your lantern and go down.

  CHAPTER I   THE EMBER HALLS   The Descent, Ember Temple, the
              Scriptorium (library wing), Cistern of Tears, the Sealed
              Crypt and the Warden's Hall.     Boss: the Bone Warden
  CHAPTER II  THE DROWNED CITY  Flooded Stair, Canal Street, Sunken
              Market, Aqueduct Works, Hall of Tides, the Pearl Vault,
              Palace Gate and the Drowned Throne. Boss: the Drowned King
  CHAPTER III THE EMBER CORE    Ash Stair, Obsidian Gallery, Lava Falls,
              Cinder Vault, Molten Bridge, Forge of the Builders, Heart
              Gate and the Ember Heart.         Boss: the Ember Wyrm
  Rekindle the Ember for the ending.

  Things to find: the Ember Staff (fire magic), keys, Reed Boots (wade
  through water), the Runeblade (+2 damage), the Sunstone (a bigger
  lantern beam - and every hidden wall glows), the Ring of Embers (walk
  on lava), heart crystals, mana crystals, potions and gems.
  Monsters: bats, skeletons, slimes, ghosts (through walls), spiders,
  eels (in the water), drowned dead, fire imps (they shoot) and magma
  slimes. Each boss has its own mode(1) portrait when you meet it.
  Kills give experience; level ups raise life and mana. If you fall you
  wake at the entrance of the room - the world keeps what you've done.

NEW IN 1.3  (art tidy)
  * Floors are plain full stops. Scenery stays in semigraphics, so any
    TEXT character you see is something to pick up or use.
  * Items are flashing ASCII (normal / inverse):
      &  key        $  gem         !  potion       +  heart crystal
      %  mana       [  reed boots  )  runeblade    *  sunstone
      O  ring of embers            /  ember staff
  * Walls are solid blocks of colour, so the lantern falls away in bands:
    bright (yellow / white) -> dim (orange / red) -> memory (blue).
  * Rooms are built halls now, not caves: the Descent, the Sealed Crypt,
    the Warden's Hall, the Flooded Stair, the Pearl Vault, the Ash Stair
    and the Ember Heart are squared up, and the Grotto of Tears is now
    the CISTERN OF TEARS (a pillared reservoir with a glowing pool).
  * Hidden walls match the walls around them (no colour giveaway).
  * New attract demo for the new Descent.

NEW IN 1.2  (v1.1 hung after "FILE TYPE BINARY")
  * Memory now works the way BIX uses your BennVenn card: the game never
    calls the SD DOS after loading. Everything is inside DEPTHS.VZ.
      - The .VZ is packed and loads low ($8000-$D7E7). A small stub
        unpacks it in RAM (like Xevious 1.2 / Cybernoid, no mapper write
        while loading, nothing unpacked from video RAM).
      - port $37 = 3 bank ($4000-$67FF): common data + current chapter
      - port $37 = 1 home bank: the three chapter blocks (this overwrites
        the DOS, as BIX and Cybernoid do - reset to load something else)
      - port $37 = 5 is not used at all.
      - Both banks are checksummed at power-on; if one fails you get
        "128K SRAM BANK TEST FAILED: 1" or ": 3" (tell me the digit).
  * The sampled speech is gone (it needed the SD card at run time and
    didn't fit in the single file).
  * New music engine, after the Cybernoid VZ title player: three voices
    (lead, harmony, bass) plus kick / snare / hat drum bursts. Every note
    starts as a square wave and narrows (BIX's settings), leads are an
    octave lower, so it is warmer and much less shrill. T switches the
    music on and off (title and in game).
  * Still in from 1.1: attract demo, portals, the pressure plate, monsters
    that back away when hurt.

CONTROLS
  Q A O P  (or W S D, or either VZ joystick)   move
  SPACE / FIRE   swing the sword (walking into a monster also strikes)
  M / ARM        cast a fireball (needs the Ember Staff and mana)
  RETURN         parchment automap (mode 1)      I   inventory
  Strike a suspicious wall: some walls are not walls.

  Title: N or FIRE  new game     C  enter a password
         S story   I how to play   K credits  (they also cycle by themselves)

PASSWORDS (instead of saving)
  Each chapter opens with a title card showing a six-letter password.
  It holds the chapter, your items, life, mana, level and gems. Press C
  on the title, type it (CTRL deletes, RETURN accepts, SPACE cancels).
  Example: TMF96K starts chapter III with everything.
  Why passwords: the BennVenn DOS calls I know ($5F00 open, $5F03 first
  sector, $5F09 next sector) only read. If you know a write call, the
  game could save to SD instead.

HOW IT USES THE MACHINE
  * Mode(0) for play, with the text colour set per region (warm brown
    floors in temples, crypts and volcanoes; green moss in the caves and
    the drowned city); mode(1) for the automap, boss portraits and the
    ending picture.
  * Directional lantern: a bright cone where you face, a dim ring and a
    glow all round, cast through precomputed shadow-casting trees.
    Torches, braziers, candles, crystals, luminous pools, lava, magic
    seals, fireballs and explosions all light the scene; walls block
    light; what you've seen stays as a blue memory map.
  * Memory:  $7E00 loader stub (power-on only)   $8000-$C3A2 code+tables
             $C800 chapter state   $CC00-$F8FF level map (tile + light)
             port $37 = 3 bank: $4000 common data, $5A40 current chapter
             port $37 = 1 home bank: chapter archive (DOS overwritten)
  * Interrupts are off; the game syncs to field sync. Music plays in the
    idle time of every frame (title, chapter cards and boss portraits get
    almost the whole frame; in play the game logic takes a share).

IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG - what to tell me
  * Screen stays on the loader text after FILE TYPE BINARY: the stub /
    unpacking (a photo helps).
  * "128K SRAM BANK TEST FAILED: n": which digit.
  * To jump straight to the later chapters (tests the chapter archive):
      29TZE9  chapter II  (staff, level 3)
      TLAJX5  chapter III (staff, boots, sunstone, runeblade, level 4)
      TMF96K  chapter III with everything

NOT IN THIS VERSION
  Split-screen status bar, saving (passwords instead), sampled speech.
