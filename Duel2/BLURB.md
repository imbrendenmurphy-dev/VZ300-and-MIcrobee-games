# DUEL II — ACE OF ACES RETURNS
### for the Dick Smith VZ200 / VZ300 — ViBED by BPM 2026

**Tally ho!** The little VZ dogfight is back. Two biplanes, one wrap-around sky and a
handful of clouds to hide in. Bank, throttle back to tighten the turn, and get on your
rival's tail. Hit him fifteen times before he does it to you.

The **Campaign** pits you against eight computer aces across four theatres:

1. **The Meadows**: Rookie Rudy and Farmer Fritz, over patchwork fields
2. **The Ocean**: Salty Sue and Captain Kraken, above a cyan sea
3. **The Desert**: Dune Raider and Scorpion, with gusts that blow you sideways
4. **Night Storm**: the Night Owl and the dreaded Phantom, as lightning crackles from the clouds

Every ace has a face, a catchphrase and a style of their own. The Phantom vanishes into
cloud when the fight turns against him.

Shoot the drifting supply balloons for **Repair**, **Twin Guns**, **Turbo** or a **Shield**.
Between theatres, burst twenty balloons against the clock in **Balloon Bust**. Beat all
eight for the **ACE OF ACES** medal.

Or go head to head in a **two-player duel** on the original's own keys. Press **C** on the
menu for **CLASSIC 1984**: the green sky and yellow puffs of the game that started it all.

It all wraps up in:
- a big-logo title with two aces dogfighting across it
- a cheeky two-voice title theme, plus jingles for every round, duel and power-up
- smoke trails, explosions, parachutes and lightning
- an engine that drones with your throttle
- an attract sequence with a computer v computer demo
- a Hall of Fame that remembers you
