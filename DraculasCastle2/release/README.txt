for the Dick Smith VZ300 with the BennVenn SD loader + 128K RAM expansion
Playtested on real VZ300 hardware.

INSTALLING
  Copy EVERYTHING in the SD_CARD folder (DRACULA2.VZ and all 38 DC2*.DAT
  files) to the ROOT of your SD card - not inside a folder - then load and
  run DRACULA2.VZ from the BennVenn menu like any other .VZ program.
  The game loads its artwork, maps and music from the .DAT files as you play.

  If it stops with "CAN'T LOAD GAME FILE", a .DAT file is missing or isn't
  in the root of the card. extras/SDTEST.VZ checks the card for you.

CONTROLS
  W or I ............ step forward (hold to keep walking)
  A/J, D/L .......... turn left / right
  S or K ............ turn around
  SPACE or RETURN ... act: talk, open, search, read / select in menus
  M ................. automap
  E ................. status & inventory (1 = drink a potion)
  N ................. music on/off (works anywhere)
  P ................. pause (Q = quit to title)
  Joystick works on either port (fire = SPACE).

  In battle: ATTACK, MAGIC (fire), ITEM, DEFEND or RUN.
  Save your game at the stone angels; CONTINUE on the title screen
  picks up where you left off.

TIPS
  Silver weapons, holy water and fire hurt the undead more.
  Shopkeepers: press 1, 2 or 3 to buy, any other key to leave.
  Look for a brass plaque in the Great Hall...
