FRILLSEEKER - THE RED DIRT AND STONE COUNTRY
for the Microbee Premium Plus (1MB RAM, 32K PCG)
VIBED BY BPM 2026

At the A> prompt type:   FRILL

C = colour monitor, M = monochrome monitor,
then SPACE at the title.

LEFT/RIGHT or O/P   run
SPACE               jump (hold for higher)
UP or Q             the frill: on the ground it frightens
                    toads on to their backs; in the air it
                    is a parachute
DOWN + SPACE        drop through a branch
KEEP RUNNING        Bluff bolts: longer leaps,
                    and he can run on water
A joystick works too (fire = jump, up = frill).

A toad on its back is a trampoline. Eat the bugs.

Magpies swoop: bluff them. Goannas are not
impressed: jump them, climb, or run.

Two regions, twelve rooms, 34 bugs. Lose your
hearts and try again from the start of the
region; ESC goes back to the title.
