SOUTHERN CROSS - Stage 4: missions
Microbee Premium Plus (32K PCG)          VIBED BY BPM 2026
===========================================================

RUNNING
  Boot this disk and type SCROSS at the prompt. It loads
  CORAL.LOC (Coral Coast) and opens on the mission menu.
  SCROSS name loads name.LOC instead (later areas).
  The logbook CORAL.LOG is written to this disk after each
  mission, so leave the disk unprotected. RESET to exit.

MISSION MENU
  1-5      pick a mission (briefing, then SPACE to fly)
  F        free flight (all four airfields)
  In flight M goes back to the menu.

MISSIONS (CORAL COAST)
  1 FIRST SOLO      circuit gates, then land on 36
  2 LIGHTHOUSE RUN  low gates up the coast and headland
  3 ISLAND HOPPER   land at Turtle Island, then Hope Island
  4 SUPPLY SHIP     low pass along the ship, land at home
  5 REEF PATROL     four reef gates, then land at Hope Is

  Gates are 60 m squares between two heights. The line
  under the instruments shows the gate, its distance,
  which way to turn and the height band (CLIMB/LOWER/OK).
  A miss costs nothing: come round again. A crash (or R)
  puts you back at the last gate with 15 seconds added.
  Rating against par: ACE AVIATOR, CAPTAIN, FIRST OFFICER,
  STUDENT PILOT.

FLYING KEYS
  Q / A    nose down / nose up
  O / P    bank left / right (steer when on the ground)
  T / G    throttle up / down (throttle 0 = brakes)
  SPACE    level the wings
  R        runway (in a mission: back to the last gate)
  F        final approach        (free flight)
  N        next airfield, final  (free flight)
  D        detail 1-3 (starts at 2; 3 adds the ground grid)
  M        mission menu

LANDING
  Full throttle, nose up with A at about 52 kt, climb at
  60-70 kt. Stall is about 43 kt. On approach keep the PAPI
  at WWRR (W = white, R = red), touch down at the markers
  under ~300 ft/min with the wings level for a good grade.
  A firm arrival bounces; only a real crash restarts you.
